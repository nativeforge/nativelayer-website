Licenses

How nativelayer packages, tools, and products may be used. Commercial licenses are offered at early access pricing while the stack is in active development.

- Non-commercial use
- Commercial use

Non-commercial use

Free under PolyForm Noncommercial for:

  • Open-source projects (OSI-approved licenses)
  • Personal projects and non-revenue applications
  • Education and academic research
  • Non-profit organizations
  • Internal tools that do not generate revenue
  • Prototypes and MVPs (non-revenue)

Commercial use

A commercial license is required for:

  • Proprietary and closed-source commercial software
  • SaaS and revenue-generating applications
  • Client work delivered to paying customers
  • Enterprise deployments at scale
  • White-label products licensed to third parties
